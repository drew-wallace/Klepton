# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

This policy covers the **HL1VR**, **HL2VR**, and **P1VR** applications for Apple
Vision Pro, which are launchers for the open-source Klepton runtime.

## What we collect

We do not collect personal data from these apps. App settings and optional
guardian boundaries are stored locally on your device; they are not sent to us
or shared with third parties.

- No account, login, or sign-up is required.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising identifiers are used.
- No data is sent to us or to any third party.

## How the apps work

The apps run entirely on your device. Game content is loaded from files that
**you** provide locally on the device; the apps do not require a network
connection to function. Any files you select stay on your device.

## Guardian boundaries

If you choose a drawn or mapped guardian, the app requests World Sensing access
to detect the floor, room and nearby surfaces. Environment geometry is used on
the device to define the play area.

Drawn and automatic boundary outlines and their spatial-anchor identifiers are saved locally
so they can be reused when you return to the same physical area. visionOS manages
the persistent spatial anchors. You can remove the saved outlines and anchors
using **Forget saved boundaries** in the Guardian panel.

## Children's privacy

We do not collect personal data from children.
